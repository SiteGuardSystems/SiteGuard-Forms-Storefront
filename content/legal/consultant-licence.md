It sits alongside the Terms of Sale, the FORMS Terms of Use and the Document Use Disclaimer. Where this licence and the Terms of Sale differ on a consultant subscription, this licence applies.

### 1. What you are licensed to do

1. **Prepare documents for your clients.** While your subscription is active, you may generate documents from the SiteGuard library included in your tier, filled with a client's details and logo, and supply them to that client for use in that client's own business.
2. **White-label.** You may supply documents under your client's name and logo. You may also supply them under your own name, provided the SiteGuard reference, revision and footer details on each document are not removed or altered.
3. **Adapt.** You and your clients may edit the documents you supply so they fit the client's work and sites.
4. **Charge your clients.** You set your own fees to your clients for your services and the documents you prepare. SiteGuard has no say in your pricing.

3. Share your login, or let people other than your own staff and engaged contractors use your account.
4. Remove the Document Use Disclaimer from a set, or tell a client that a document is certified, compliant or approved by SiteGuard or any regulator.
5. Supply documents outside your tier's scope. **Documents held from self-serve supply (including the Office of the Federal Safety Commissioner suite) are not included in any consultant tier** until SiteGuard announces otherwise in writing.
