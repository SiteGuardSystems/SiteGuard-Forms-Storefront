1. **Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.** Our products come with consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded. If a document has a major failure (for example, it is materially different from its description, or it can't be opened or used as supplied), you're entitled to a replacement or a refund. For a minor failure, we'll fix or replace it within a reasonable time.
2. **Change of mind.** Our products are digital and can be copied once delivered, so we don't offer change-of-mind refunds after an order has been delivered. If you cancel an order **before** we deliver it, we'll refund it in full.
3. **Wrong item ordered.** If you ordered the wrong package and contact us within **[7] days** of delivery, we may, at our discretion, swap it for the correct package and charge or refund the difference.
4. **Subscriptions.** You can cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. We don't give part-period refunds, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires one or we have materially changed the subscription to your detriment.
5. **How to ask.** Email **[support email]** with your order number. We respond within **[2] business days**. Refunds go back to the original payment method.
